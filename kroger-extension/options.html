DSB Recipe Book → Kroger

Send your grocery list from your recipe book, then add it to your kroger.com cart in one click.

1 Your Kroger app

Kroger only lets apps it knows about add to carts, so this extension uses a free app you register once.

  1. Sign in at developer.kroger.com (your normal Kroger account works) and click Create App.
  2. Name it something like “DSB Recipe Book”. Under the APIs, include Products, Locations and Cart.
